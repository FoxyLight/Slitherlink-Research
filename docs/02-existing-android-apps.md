# 02 — Existing Android apps

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-29  
**Evidence boundary:** Google Play store listings and publishers' sites were inspected. **No apps were installed or hands-on tested.** Download bands are store-reported, not active-user counts. Claims of puzzle uniqueness and hint quality remain unverified.

## Established observations from published material

- Existing products already advertise large or unlimited supplies of puzzles.
- Offline play, hints, dark modes, difficulty options, and zoom are not new differentiators.
- Conceptis emphasizes manually selected puzzles; Ejelta and Tatham emphasize generated content.
- Multiple apps already offer loop-related visual aids and assistance.
- Two apps reporting the same feature tells us little about how well that feature actually works.

Sources: individual store links in the table; [Ejelta explanation](https://ejelta.com/slitherlink/); [Tatham puzzle collection](https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/puzzles/).

## Prior review leads — not independently rechecked

An earlier exploratory report noted individual reviewer requests about drag controls, removing obsolete X marks, tentative lines, shading, and intrusive completion advertising. These should be treated as **unverified research leads until each review is captured with its store URL, date, quoted scope, and context**. They do not establish prevalence or a market gap.

## What to test by hand

Use the same small, medium, and large puzzles on a real Android device and record:

1. Tap-to-mark and drag behavior, accidental selections and reversals.
2. Distinguishing scrolling/panning from board editing.
3. Small-screen legibility and zoom stability.
4. Crosses, tentative lines, error feedback, and hints.
5. Persistence after backgrounding, rotation, and process restart.
6. Offline behavior and any interruptions.
7. Whether difficulty descriptions match observed complexity.

Maintain a reproducible comparison sheet with app version, device, Android version, puzzle identifiers, exact tasks, evidence screenshots, and raw observations.

## Provisional conclusion

Better input precision, reliable logical hints, and puzzle quality are possible research directions, **not demonstrated unmet needs**. Do not choose a standout feature until observational testing.
